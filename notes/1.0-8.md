# Dict 1.0 (build 8)

## AirPods work properly now

If you dictate with AirPods, or switch between AirPods and another microphone, this build is for you.

- **No more crashes when you change mics.** Taking AirPods out and dictating with your Mac's built-in mic or a USB mic used to crash Dict on the next keypress. Dict now reads the microphone's current format every time it starts recording instead of trusting what it saw last time, which is what the crash came down to.
- **The waveform appears right away.** Bluetooth mics take about a second to switch on, and Dict used to wait for that before showing anything. Now the HUD comes up immediately and says **CONNECTING** until your AirPods are actually listening.
- **Recordings don't go silent.** When a mic changes its settings mid-sentence, Dict picks the recording back up within a fraction of a second instead of recording silence until you let go.

Other microphones get the same treatment, so plugging one in or unplugging it between dictations is safe too.
