# Dict 1.0 (build 11)

## Light mode

Dict has always been dark. Now you can pick. Open Settings and look for **Appearance** in General:

- **Dark** is the look you already have, and it stays the default.
- **Light** gives the Dict window and the dictation panel a bright, clean look.
- **Match System** switches between dark and light along with your Mac.

Light mode works with all five color schemes. The dictation panel turns white, and the dots light up in deeper versions of your scheme's colors so they're easy to see. Graphite switches to dark gray dots on white.

The change applies right away, including to the dictation panel, so you can try each option and see what you like.
