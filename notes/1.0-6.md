# Dict 1.0 (build 6)

## Dict is now a paid app

Dict costs $9.99, once. There's no subscription and no account, and one purchase covers all of your Macs.

- **14 days to try it.** Your free trial starts the first time you open this version, and every feature works during it. The menu bar menu shows how many days are left.
- **When the trial ends,** dictation stops until you enter a license key. Your settings, word replacements and history all stay where they are.
- **Buying takes a minute.** Choose Buy Dict in the menu bar menu or in Settings under License. Checkout opens in your browser at dictapp.app. When it's done, click Activate in Dict on the confirmation page and Dict unlocks.
- **Your key stays on your Mac.** Dict checks the key against a public key built into the app. It never sends your key anywhere or asks a server whether it's valid, so Dict still never goes online except to check for updates and to download the Parakeet model if you choose it.
- **Setting up another Mac?** Settings, License has Copy License Key. Paste the key into Dict on the other Mac, or choose Enter License Key in the menu.

Thank you for trying Dict while it was free. If you have questions about the change, email matt@birchtree.me.
