# Dict 1.0 (build 10)

## Long dictations come out cleaner in Verbatim

Verbatim is still instant when you hold the key. When you tap Space to go hands-free, which is usually when you're dictating something longer like an email or a blog post, Dict now tidies the text before it goes in:

- **Names get capitalized.** People, places, companies, and products come out the way they're usually written, like "iPhone" and "YouTube".
- **Punctuation is smarter,** including question marks when you ask something.
- **Ums and stumbles are removed.** Filler sounds like "um" and "uh" go away, and so do words you tripped over and repeated, like "the the".

Your wording doesn't change. Dict checks the result word by word, and if anything other than capitals, punctuation, fillers, or repeats changed, it uses exactly what you said instead. Words like "like" and "you know" stay, and so do your own corrections.

Tidying uses the on-device Apple Intelligence model, so expect a short pause after a long hands-free dictation. Coding apps keep working the way they did.
