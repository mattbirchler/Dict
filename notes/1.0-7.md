# Dict 1.0 (build 7)

## Insert a dictation again from the menu bar

Dictated while the wrong field was focused? Click into the right one, open the Dict menu, and choose **Insert Last Dictation**. The text goes in with the same spacing a new dictation would get.

- **Recent Dictations** lists your last five, so you can put any of them back without opening History. **Show All** opens the full list.
- **Hold Option** while the menu is open to copy instead of insert.

## Anonymous usage data

Dict now sends a few anonymous counts, like how many words a dictation had and which engine you use. They show how much Dict gets used and where people get stuck setting it up.

- **Never sent:** your audio, your words, your history, your word replacements, or your license key. Your IP address and location aren't stored either.
- **Turn it off** in Settings, General, Share anonymous usage data. Nothing is sent after that.

The full list of what's sent is in the [privacy statement](https://github.com/mattbirchler/Dict-releases/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).
