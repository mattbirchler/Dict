# Dict 1.0 (build 5)

## Fixes

- **AirPods work as a microphone.** Choosing AirPods recorded nothing, and the app could crash on your next key press. AirPods switch to a different audio format when their mic turns on, and Dict now follows that change instead of tripping over it. If a mic changes format mid-recording, Dict picks the recording back up.

## Improvements

- **You can see when the speech model is loading.** Right after launch, Parakeet takes a moment to load. The menu bar icon now animates while that happens, and the menu says "Loading speech model…". When it finishes, a short "Ready to dictate" notice appears at the bottom of the screen so you know you can start talking.
- **Names come out the way your document spells them, with any engine.** Dict reads the field you're typing into and fixes names and terms to match it. "birchtree" becomes "Birchtree", "iphone" becomes "iPhone", and a name heard as two words ("birch tree") is joined back up. Before, this only worked when Apple Intelligence formatting ran, which with Parakeet was mostly in coding apps. Ordinary words are left alone, so a document that mentions "Mark" won't capitalize every "mark". You can turn this off in Settings under "Spell names the way the document does".
