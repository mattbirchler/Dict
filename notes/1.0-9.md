# Dict 1.0 (build 9)

## Numbers come out as numbers

Say "twenty five" and you get 25. Dict used to write most numbers out in words, and this build fixes that in every dictation style, including Verbatim.

- **Everyday numbers:** "three point five" becomes 3.5, "fifteen thousand" becomes 15,000, "three million" stays readable as 3 million.
- **Dates and times:** "nineteen eighty four" becomes 1984, "the twenty first" becomes the 21st, and "meet at three thirty" becomes 3:30.
- **Numbers read one digit at a time** join up, so a phone number or zip code comes out as one number.

Dict still leaves words alone when they aren't really counts. "This one" and "one of them" keep their "one", and "the first thing" stays the first thing.
